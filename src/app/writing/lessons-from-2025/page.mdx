# Somewhere between a checklist and the cosmos.

_Head of International Press · MUN 2026_

I make lists. A lot of lists. In fact, I’m surprised I don’t leave little paper trails of scribbles everywhere I go. If you’re looking for the human personification of order in the chaos, look no further.

When I’m not occupied with my list-making, you’ll find me outside with my trusty old *Van Gogh’s Starry Night* journal, drawing. Everything valuable I’ve ever learnt comes from questioning, and so I’m always willing to push the boundaries and discuss just about anything under the sun—with an especial penchant for scientific paradoxes, aerospace, and philosophy.

I see life as the cosmos, with us in a ship surrounded by a billion other stars. To navigate it, we all must ask ourselves where we want to go.

And that, is me stuck in a writer’s curse.
